26.10.20Objects and Attention

A useful object does not always need to ask for attention. Sometimes its best quality is how easily it settles into a routine.

A switch that makes sense without instructions, a light that tells you just enough, a speaker that belongs in the room. These small decisions are worth noticing.

26.09.15Before Adding Another Feature

Before adding a feature, I like to return to the moment of use. Where is the person? What are their hands doing? What are they actually trying to finish?

A product can gain clarity when a decision is removed. The question is not how many things it can do, but how comfortably it fits the situation.
