ZYAN Han Serif Light is a web subset of Source Han Serif CN Light by Adobe,
from https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif (release branch).
The outlines and Light weight are unchanged. This subset contains the Chinese
characters and punctuation used by this site. Its internal family name was
changed to ZYAN Han Serif Light. The font is distributed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1; see OFL.txt for copyright and license terms.
