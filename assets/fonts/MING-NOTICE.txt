ZYAN Ming ExtraLight is a web subset of Source Han Serif SC ExtraLight by Adobe,
from https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif at commit
7889f11bf31170b5d092a083b357c8c8130f89e0.
The outlines and ExtraLight weight are unchanged. It contains the Simplified
Chinese characters and punctuation used by this site. Its internal family name
was changed to ZYAN Ming ExtraLight. It is distributed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1; see OFL.txt for copyright and license terms.
The site consistently uses this self-hosted font for CJK characters. No local
MingLiU/PMingLiU is preferred and no proprietary font file is redistributed.
Rebuild and verify coverage with scripts/build-font.py; see SITE-SPEC.md.
