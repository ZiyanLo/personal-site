ZYAN Ming ExtraLight is a web subset of Source Han Serif TW ExtraLight by Adobe,
from https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif (release branch).
The outlines and ExtraLight weight are unchanged. It contains the Traditional
Chinese characters and punctuation used by this site. Its internal family name
was changed to ZYAN Ming ExtraLight. It is distributed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1; see OFL.txt for copyright and license terms.
The site uses a locally installed MingLiU/PMingLiU when available, and this
open-source font as the web fallback. No MingLiU font file is redistributed.
